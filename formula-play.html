Getting started

What does this app do?

It turns a drawing or a ringing sound into a formula, and a formula back into a drawing or a sound. Create has the drawing pad, the formula box and the Motion lab. Sound has four tools: Tuner, Tune, Voice pen and Chords. Games has the daily challenge and three more ways to play.

How do I draw a picture with several lines?

Draw one line, lift your finger, and draw the next. A drawing holds up to 8 lines. “Undo last line” removes the newest one and “Start over” clears the pad.

What are the spinning circles?

Each circle is one part of the formula: its size, how fast it turns and where it starts. Joined end to end, the circles trace your line. More circles follow the line more closely, and the slider sets how many are used.

What is signature mode?

Sign your name on the pad, then press “Keep replaying”. The circles redraw your signature again and again until you stop them.

Motion lab

What is the Motion lab?

Five motions worked out from the laws of physics: a pendulum, a dropped ball, a thrown ball, a weight on a spring and a spinning wheel. It plays like a video, with the time, position, speed and acceleration written on the picture as it moves.

How do I change it?

Use the sliders and the gravity buttons under “Change it”, or drag the object on the picture: drag the pendulum to set its angle, the dropped ball to set its height, the spring weight to set how far it is pulled, and away from the throw's start point to aim the throw.

What do the arrows and dots mean?

The blue arrow is the velocity: where the object is heading and how fast. The amber arrow is the acceleration. Longer means bigger. The orange dots are where the object was at equal steps of time, so dots far apart mean it was moving fast.

Is this a real video?

No. It is worked out from the formulas, so every number is exact for the model. The model leaves out air drag and friction, so a real ball would slow a little more.

Sending

How does sending work?

“Send to a friend” opens your phone's share menu with a link. The link holds the formula itself, so your friend sees or hears it as soon as they open it. If the share menu is not available, the link is copied for you to paste.

What is a formula code?

The same formula as a short piece of text starting with FP1. Use it where links do not work. Your friend opens Menu, then “Open a formula code”, and pastes it.

How does the guessing game work?

Before you send a drawing, type what it is in the box on the Send card. Your friend watches the circles draw it slowly and types a guess. The earlier the right guess, the higher the score.

Sound

The microphone does nothing

The microphone only works when the app is opened from its own web address, and after you allow it when your phone asks. Inside a preview it is switched off. You can still use the sounds made by the app, or pick a sound file.

How does the background noise filter work?

It removes deep rumble and hum below 75 Hz and hiss above 4,000 Hz, keeps track of how loud the background is, and only counts a sound that is well above it and has a clear pitch. Readings are combined over a fraction of a second so one noisy moment does not make the note jump. In a very noisy place, choose “Strong”, which also uses your phone's own noise reduction, and hold the phone close to the sound.

Why does it say “no clear note”?

A formula needs a sound that rings or hums: a string, a glass, a whistle, a held voice. Thuds, clicks, speech and background noise have no single pitch, so the app says so instead of guessing.

How do I hum a tune?

Press “Record 8 seconds” and hum or whistle one note after another, each held for a moment. Sliding between notes makes them harder to tell apart.

Games and scores

How is my drawing scored?

Two things count: how much of the real curve your line covers, and how close your line stays to it. Using the hint caps the score at 70.

How do levels and the run of days work?

In Practice, three scores of 80 or more without the hint take you to the next level, up to level 5. A day counts toward your run when you score 50 or more on that day's challenge.

How does a duel work?

After a challenge or practice result, send it from “Challenge a friend”. Your friend gets the same formula and your score to beat, and the app says who won.

Your data

Does anything leave my phone?

No account is needed and nothing is uploaded. Drawings and sounds are worked out on your phone. They go somewhere only when you send a link or a code yourself.

Where are my level, run of days and saved creations kept?

In this browser on this phone. Clearing the browser's data, or using another phone, starts them again. Settings has a button to clear them yourself.
