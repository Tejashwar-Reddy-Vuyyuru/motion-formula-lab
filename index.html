    How it works
    1. Tap a tile. If yours is not listed, use “Any moving object”.
    2. The app checks the shot first: phone steady, upright, enough light. When it is ready it counts down and records.
    3. After recording, you tap the object once on the recorded picture. The app follows it frame by frame and builds a table of time and position.
    4. It looks for the simplest formula that fits, then shows a replay, a 3D view, graphs, forces and energy, and a time slider to predict where the object will be.
    5. If nothing fits cleanly it gives a rough result with a warning, or says there is no formula and what to change. It does not guess.
